🕹 Controls

SPACE or CLICK / TAP — Flap wings (Applies upward impulse force)

🎯 Objectives

Navigate through pipe gap intervals to score points. Reach the target score to clear each stage!

⚠️ Environmental Hazards

• Avoid collisions with pipe structures.
• Do not crash into the ground surface.
• In Level 2 & 3, watch out for vertically moving and predictive tracking obstacles!

📐 Engine Mathematics

Employs Cartesian $(X, Y)$ coordinates, 2D velocity vectors $\vec{v} = (v_x, v_y)$, Euler integration for gravity acceleration, and Circle-to-AABB bounding box collision checks.
